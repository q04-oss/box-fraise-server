Without community, nothing can be built.

The core technology of the platform is the scannable advertisement, and it is already functioning in the wild.

There is no code printed on it. You point your camera at the whole page and the platform recognises it.

The next step is building the run club, and getting businesses interested in advertising to it.

The fee a business pays covers the platform and gives a few dollars to everybody who witnesses the advertisement. Box Fraise is promoting consensual advertising.

After that, the app. A map, and a runner is a pin. The run is geotagged, so a business can buy a kilometre of it.
