Just turn up. There is nothing to sign and nobody to email first. Come at 7:40, run at 8:30, have a coffee after.

Why a cafe needs a run club. The espresso bar is one of the few genuinely democratic meeting spaces left: anyone can stand at it, and standing at it is enough to be part of what is happening there. A run club gives a cafe a reason to gather people rather than only serve them.

What it is part of. Box Fraise is the next generation of Playboy magazine, and an attempt to imagine what the next generation of business school is going to be. The run is the first thing it does — a community organised around what is good for the nervous system, on the theory that better people make better work. The whole argument is here.
