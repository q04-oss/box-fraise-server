Games

Two games about the same street.

Played from opposite sides of the same argument.

Whyte

You run down Whyte Avenue and the obstacles are cars. That is not a theme — it is the street as it is now.

One button · a leaderboard · watch other people play

Stand

You send people into the road. Whoever survives becomes part of the street, until there is no lane left to drive down.

One button · the street as it could be

Both were made by a run club that meets three times a week on two streets and wants both of them closed to cars. There is a desktop with them on it, too.
