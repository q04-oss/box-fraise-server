Box Fraise clubhouse

The rule is you run every morning.

A place for a group of runners to sleep and live, a hair salon, a hot yoga studio. It does not exist yet.

Miss one and nothing happens. You run the next morning. There is no penalty and nothing is taken away.

It operates like a monastery. Not a school. Nobody enrols, there is no course, and nobody graduates. There is a rule, a roof, and the people keeping it.

What is in it.

  • 01A place for a group of runners to sleep and live.
  • 02A hair salon.
  • 03A hot yoga studio.

Who joins. People without housing join the ranks, on the same terms as everybody else: turn up to a run, and somebody makes you an account while you are standing there. There is no application and no assessment.

You get the same number as everybody else. Numbers are given in the order people joined and there is no other kind. Nothing about a number says who sleeps in the building.

Leaving. You stay while you find work. You stay after that until you have savings. When you are ready you leave and rent somewhere — still part of the community, still the same number.

Two different runs. The clubhouse runs every morning. The club runs Friday at six and Sunday at eight, and anyone can come to those.

Being sexy requires being socially conscious. Playboy had a foundation and a civil rights record, and they were not an apology for the magazine — they were part of why it was worth reading. Box Fraise is the next generation of Playboy magazine, and this is that part of it. It is a house rather than a donation. The whole argument is here.

What the money is for. This. It is the only thing Box Fraise is raising money to build.

Two ways to put money in. Both in person and both cash, at the run club, Friday evenings and Sunday mornings.

  • $5Buy a strawberry sticker. Put it wherever you like.
  • ANYOr leave a donation. Any amount.

In person, like everything else. You cannot donate from a sofa any more than you can join from one. Turning up is the whole system, and this is not an exception to it.

Box Fraise is not a registered charity, so neither is tax-deductible. There is no online payment.
