Do you have what it takes to run this country?

Playboy was the most advanced advertising machine ever built. Box Fraise is the next generation of it — a magazine whose question is answered by running across the community it is asking about.

Next · →

There is a run club, and anyone can join it. Every Saturday at 8 on Whyte Avenue, out to the Canadian Pacific railyard and back — across the place the question is about. You do not need to be fast and you do not need to know anyone. Turn up and run.

The running is not a metaphor and the question is not rhetorical. The people who turn up are the answer.
