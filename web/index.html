Do you have what it takes to run this country?

Playboy was the most advanced advertising machine ever built. Box Fraise is the next generation of it — a magazine whose question is answered by running across the community it is asking about.

Next · →

That happens every Saturday at 8 on Whyte Avenue — out to the Canadian Pacific railyard and back, across the place the question is about. The running is not a metaphor and the question is not rhetorical: the people who turn up are the answer. Stickers are $5 there. Where, and why →

Everything the magazine makes goes out into the world for you to point a camera at. An advertisement is a page of it, scanned whole, with no code printed on it. A sticker is a pin on the map below — which is how the next person finds the run. The advertising is the community advertising itself.
