Advertise

Nobody is shown this against their will.

Box Fraise is a consensual advertising engine. Your advertisement is a printed thing somebody chooses to point a camera at, and it opens whatever you want it to open.

What you are buying. A picture, registered with the platform, that the scanner recognises. Put it in your window, on a wall, on a flyer, on the back of a till receipt. Somebody opens the scanner, points their phone at it, and lands where you sent them.

There is no code printed on it. No QR square, nothing that spoils the artwork. The platform recognises the picture itself, so what goes on the wall is your design and nothing else.

Who is at the other end. People who turned up to a run on Whyte Avenue — in person, in the morning, on purpose. That is the whole audience and it is the reason it is worth something. Ask how many there were last month; the number comes off the platform rather than out of the air.

How it goes

  • 01You send the artwork and say where a scan should land.
  • 02It is registered the same day. Nothing is rebuilt and nothing is deployed.
  • 03You print it and put it up.
  • 04It can be taken down again in a moment, and put back.
