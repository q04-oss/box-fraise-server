Stand

Whoever stays becomes part of the street.

Step into the road when it is clear. Mistime it and a car takes them.

A person who stays does not move again. They become a body in the road, then a bollard, then a table with somebody sitting at it. Each one narrows the lane. Cars brake, queue, and go another way — and when there is no lane left the street stops being a road and becomes a place.

The crowd is the score and the cost. Every person you send out spends one. Every one who stays brings another, because people gather where people already are. Run out and it stays a road.

The other way round from Whyte. There you dodge the cars, which is what the street does to you now. Here you end them, which is what the run club is asking for. Wednesday, Friday and Sunday →
