Who turned up
A membership lasts a month. Ask for their number and type it in — nothing to scan, nothing that works from a sofa. Marking somebody twice at the same run does nothing.
Sign somebody up
The only way to become a member, and it counts as their first run. No name is taken — a member is a number, given in the order people joined. They scan the code off this screen and the membership lands on their phone — it is shown once and never again, so if the scan fails, make another.
Have them point their camera at it.
Lost phone
For somebody who already has a number and cannot get in — new phone, wiped browser, signed out. This hands them a fresh code for the same account: their number, their posts and their months of turning up all stay. It is the only way back in, and it only happens here, with them standing in front of you.

Every device they had is signed out first, because the usual reason for asking is that one of them is gone. Say so out loud. Their conversations will not come back either — those keys were on the old phone and nowhere else.
Have them point their camera at it.
Magazine — sent in anonymously

Run away — anonymous answers

Advertisements
A picture in the street and what happens when a camera is pointed at it. Registering one here puts it live — no redeploy. The artwork is only ever fingerprinted, never shown, so a small file is fine.
Offers
What a member finds in their inbox. Nothing here charges anybody: the business handed over cash at a run first, and this records what it bought. The amount is per yes, and the count is how many yeses were paid for.
Money owed
Members who said yes and have not been handed the money yet. Pay in cash at a run, then mark it here — the same shape as attendance, a fact recorded by somebody who was standing there. It cannot be undone.
Advert requests
What businesses sent in from /advertise. Accepting one puts it in every inbox at the standing price — $3 an open, $1 of it to the reader — so accept it after they have paid, not before.
Adverts — the running inbox
What a signed-in runner finds in their inbox on the homepage. The line is what they see unopened; the body is what appears when they choose to open it, and is never sent to anybody who has not. Nothing here charges anybody — the amount times the count is money that already changed hands.
Owed for adverts
Runners who opened something and have not been handed the money. Pay in cash, then mark it here. It cannot be undone.
Who works where
The link a shift needs, and the one the strike counts. Businesses cannot sign in yet, so it is entered here on their behalf.
Publish a shift
What is published here is what the member sees, and it is binding. A shift can be cancelled — they will see that it was — but its times cannot be edited. A change is a cancellation and a new shift.
